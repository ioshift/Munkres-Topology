# Topology Reading Notes

Personal reading notes by **Steven Shi**, based on selected material
from Part I of James R. Munkres’ *Topology* (2nd edition).

- [Notes](notes.pdf)

## Topics covered

- Set theory, order relations and the axiom of choice.
- Topological spaces, continuous functions, and product, quotient
  and metric topologies.
- Connectedness, compactness, local compactness and nets.
- Countability and separation axioms, the Urysohn lemma and
  metrization theorem, and the Tietze extension theorem.
- The Tychonoff theorem and embeddings of manifolds.

The notes collect definitions, selected results, proofs, examples
and exercise solutions for reference.
